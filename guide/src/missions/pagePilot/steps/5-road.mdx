Copy-on-write DAX landed on November 4. Before it, a window page *was* the open extent's block:
a store through the mapping, or a `write(2)`'s copy, was the write itself. No copy, no request.

From October 14 to November 4 that design took 169 commits to dax.c alone. doc-13 counted the rest
when it was replaced: about 25k lines, 7 kernel patches, 3 of 4 TLA+ specs, about 47% of tasks
titled as bugs, 37–39 of 65 dead ends, and DAX at 0.51–0.61× no-DAX under pgbench.

Copy-on-write gave up the zero-copy write to make the window immutable. Writes go through the same
writeback path as without DAX; reads stay zero-copy, which is where DAX's value was. The perf gate's
latest recorded full run, r908, has DAX at 1.04× no-DAX, and a forked VM at 0.94×
(`docs/measurements/dax-cow-gate.md`).
