Every rule in that sequence is there because something breaks without it. `specs/dax-cow` models
the protocol in TLA+, with a page or two, one storing task, one writeback task and the host. Each
model in `specs/dax-cow/mutations` removes one rule, and TLC must find the violation that rule
prevents, or `scripts/check-tla.sh` fails.

Pick a rule to remove. Say what you think breaks, then watch the counterexample TLC found. It is a
recorded TLC run, not an animation.
